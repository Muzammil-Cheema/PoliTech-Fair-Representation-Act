## Sources
Election results from Tennessee Secretary of State (https://sos.tn.gov/elections/results#2024). See Williamson County note under precinct sources for additional information on minor election results processing in that county.

Precinct boundaries are sourced primarily via the Tennessee Comptroller of the Treasury website (https://comptroller.tn.gov/maps.html). In the following counties, additional sources were used or processing was completed:

Bledsoe County: 6-3 Herndon was combined with 6-2 Lusk to reflect 2024 boundaries available online
Carter County: Edits to National Guard, Armory, Eastside, Siam, Keenburg, Range, and Watauga based on county correspondence
Coffee County: Separate 9-2 Cedar Lane, 9-4 Cedar Lane using information available online
Cumberland County: Edits to Homestead, Linary, Lantana and Tansi precincts based on discrepancy identified using the voter file and communication with county
Davidson County: Precinct shapefile from county GIS
Fayette County: Revert to 2023 map which is available online and was in place for 2024 elections
Jackson County: Swapped 6-1 and 6-2 in the shapefile based off of conversation with county election official
McMinn County: Edited to map to match 2024 precincts available online
Williamson County: Precinct 1-4's election results are reported with different precincts depending on the election (with 1-5 for President, Senate and TN House, but with 1-3 for US Congressional District 5). Election results 1-3-4 and 1-4-5 have been changed to 1-3 and 1-5 respectively. 1-4 precinct shape combined with 1-5 to reflect how senate and presidential results reported. Precinct maps (dated January 2024) available for precincts 1-3, 1-4 and 1-5, show these precincts with total populations of 4,205, 2 and 2,869 respectively.

All counties' precinct boundaries were compared against precinct assignments from a Tennessee voter file from L2 dated February 25th, 2025. 

## Notes on Field Names:
Columns reporting votes generally follow the pattern:
One example is:
GPREDCLI
The first character is G for a general election, P for a primary, S for a special, and R for a runoff.
Characters 2 and 3 are the year of the election.
Characters 4-6 represent the office type (see list below).
Character 7 represents the party of the candidate.
Characters 8-10 are the first three letters of the candidate's last name.

## Processing Steps
After joining the precincts to their boundaries, the files were checked for precinct-district splits. Ultimately, the precinct boundary and election result files are split across three different files. The "_all_" file contains all the election results joined to precinct boundaries, but does not account for the various precinct-district splits identified in processing and as such, does not contain district assignments. The "_cong_" and "_sldl_" files contain Congressional and State House results at the most granular units for which data is available. Please note that in order to minimize superfluous splits, precinct splits are only made when a precinct contains votes for candidates in multiple districts, as such district assignments for precincts many not perfectly correspond to the districts themselves, particularly in large, uninhabited areas.

In 5 precincts (Carter-:-Hampton, Hawkins-:-2 Church Hill, Hawkins-:-3 Carter's Valley, Henry-:-2-2, and Rutherford-:-6-1), votes for State House District candidates are present, despite the fact that this precinct does not geographically intersect that district. We confirmed that no processing errors were made in the election results and rechecked precinct boundary data online. When constructing the "_sldl_" file, these 82 votes ('GSL03DLOV': 4, 'GSL03RHIL': 22,'GSL04RJON': 23,'GSL09RHIC': 14,'GSL34DPAN': 4,'GSL34RRUD': 7,'GSL76IHAR': 1,'GSL76RDAR': 7) are removed from the file. These votes are not removed from the "_all_" file.

The "smart_repair" function from the maup package, created by MGGG (https://github.com/mggg/maup) was used to clean any minor overlap and alignment issues resulting from using a multitude of different precinct shapefile sources.

Election results were aggregated to the state-level and compared against official election results from the state (https://sos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com/s3fs-public/document/20241105GeneralbyOffice.pdf). Totals matched in all instances.
